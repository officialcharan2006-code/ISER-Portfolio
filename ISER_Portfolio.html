R. Jai Charan

Founder & Lead Researcher — Final Year B.Com (General)

ISER is an independent research initiative examining how systemic, structural change — not isolated interventions — can reshape learning outcomes. The work here approaches education the way a commerce lens approaches an economy: as a system of incentives, resource flows, and institutions, where reform has to account for cost, capacity, and behaviour, not just intent.

What ISER works on

Research Vision

Access & Equity

Structural barriers to learning

Studying how resource distribution, geography, and institutional design create uneven access to quality education — and where policy can close the gap without adding cost.

Institutional Design

Incentives inside education systems

Applying an economic lens to how schools, boards, and administrations are structured — where misaligned incentives quietly work against learning outcomes.

Applied Reform

From framework to field

Translating research findings into practical, low-cost interventions that institutions can actually adopt, rather than proposals that stay on paper.
